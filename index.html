01 / The idea

No commission doesn't
mean no responsibility.

The goal is simple: help riders and drivers find each other, agree a fare and keep the platform from taking a percentage.

Phones can handle the interface, GPS, fare offers and their owner's trip history. A small shared service can handle discovery, ride requests and one-driver-only acceptance.

The proposed funding model is separate from the fare: a driver cooperative, donations or sponsorship could cover costs. None of these is secured. Open-source software and clear control rules are goals, not claims about an existing organization.

This is an independent concept, not an operating transport company or an affiliation with any ride-hailing provider.
