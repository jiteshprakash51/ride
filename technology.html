02 / The approach

Keep the mission.
Be honest about the technology.

Internet access is necessary. It doesn't make every phone reachable, discoverable or awake.

Experimental path

Phones only

Use participants' devices for discovery and connection support. Worth testing, but not a dependable public-service promise.

  • Finding unknown nearby drivers still needs a discovery mechanism.
  • Direct connections need signaling; some network combinations need a relay.
  • Phones that sleep, disconnect or run out of battery weaken the network.

Best used for a bounded connection experiment.

Recommended direction

Small shared backend

Phones + a small service

Keep most work local. Use a small backend for the jobs that need a reachable, consistent coordinator.

  • Phones: interface, location permissions, fare negotiation and local history.
  • Backend: find online drivers, deliver requests and confirm exactly one acceptance.
  • Rider pays driver directly. No platform fare deduction in the proposed model.

Relaxes "no server", preserves "no per-ride commission".
