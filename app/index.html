    HONEST LIMITS / OPEN SOURCE

    Direct does not mean
    no trade-offs.

    No hidden backend

    Static files on Cloudflare Pages. Manual offer/answer codes replace a signaling service. Journey messages travel over an encrypted WebRTC data channel, not a database or blockchain.

    Not a citywide marketplace

    You must already have a way to contact your partner. No background dispatch, public matching, accounts or automatic reconnection. Some networks cannot connect without a relay. No TURN service is configured.

    Temporary by default

    App journey data stays in the current tabs. Disconnecting or reloading clears it here, but your peer can copy it. Pairing codes contain connection metadata; do not post them publicly. Offline mode only opens this app shell.

    Not a safety guarantee

    No driver, vehicle, insurance or legal verification is performed. Keep testing fictional. In any future service, direct cash or licensed local payment is separate. This app takes no commission and handles no money.
